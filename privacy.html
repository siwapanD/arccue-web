Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

ArcCue ("the app") is built as a local-network remote control for your Mac. We designed it so that your data stays on your devices. This policy explains what the app touches and what never leaves your hardware.

What ArcCue does NOT collect

  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • No accounts or sign-ups — we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not see, store, or transmit what you type, point at, or present.
  • We do not collect crash logs to any server of our own.

Data the app processes locally

  • Pairing & session tokens. A pairing code and a session token are exchanged between your iPhone and your Mac over your local Wi-Fi. The token is stored in the iOS Keychain (and only as a salted hash on the Mac) so the app can reconnect to a trusted Mac.
  • Motion sensor data. Gyro/attitude samples are read on the iPhone solely to move the pointer, streamed only to your Mac on the same network, and never persisted.
  • Device info. Device name, model, battery level, and app version are shared with the paired Mac over the same local connection to display connection status. They never leave your network.

Purchases

ArcCue Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We receive no card details and cannot see your payment information. Purchase status is provided to the app by Apple's StoreKit.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — only to scan the pairing QR code shown on your Mac.
  • Motion & orientation — only for the air pointer feature.
  • Local Network — to talk to the ArcCue app on your Mac.

Data security

Connections are restricted to your local network and gated by a 6-digit pairing code plus a session token. Traffic on your Wi-Fi is not additionally TLS-encrypted in the current version; do not use ArcCue on untrusted public Wi-Fi.

Children's privacy

ArcCue is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Questions? Reach us via the ArcCue website.
